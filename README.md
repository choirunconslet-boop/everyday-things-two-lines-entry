Entry for the make.fun bounty **"Everyday Things, Two Lines, Longer Run"**
(`u-suga-everyday-things-two-lines-longer-run-f8734479`).

`main.ts` writes exactly two lines to `/output/entry.txt`:
line 1 explains a toaster to a 10-year-old through a single vivid
analogy — a tiny tanning bed for bread — and line 2 says honestly
what that analogy maps (heat on both sides, the timed pop-up) and
what it leaves out (bread can't feel the heat or ask to stop, so
the toaster can burn it black).
